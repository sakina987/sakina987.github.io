The value of better words

A clearer message.
A better reason to buy.

Good copy helps people understand your offer, feel more confident, and know what to do next.

01 / MAKE IT RELEVANT

“This sounds like me.”

Speak to what your customer wants, instead of listing everything your business does.

02 / MAKE IT CLEAR

“Now I understand.”

Turn features into useful benefits. Answer the questions that can hold a purchase back.

03 / MAKE IT EASY

“I know what to do.”

Give your reader a focused next step, from exploring an offer to returning to their cart.

Better copy can support sales. Results also depend on your offer, audience, delivery and customer experience; no sales increase is guaranteed.

    Before we begin

    A few good
    questions.

    Can better copy help my sales?

    Clear, relevant copy can help customers understand an offer and take action. Sales also depend on your product, audience, pricing and email delivery. I don't promise a particular uplift; we start with a clear goal and work toward it.

    What exactly do you deliver?

    Email copy: subject lines, preview text, body copy and calls to action, as agreed in your project brief. Email design, sending, list management, automation setup and reporting are not included unless separately agreed.

    What does a project cost?

    I'll quote after understanding the number of emails, research and scope. The quote will set out the deliverables, revision rounds and timeline before work starts.

    Can I request a free email review?

    Yes — ask about current availability. Share the text of up to three emails with personal or customer details removed. If accepted, I'll confirm a delivery date and send a short note with suggested improvements. No passwords or customer lists are needed.

    Does choosing a date book a call?

    No. The calendar adds your preferred project date to your project brief. I'll confirm availability with you. It is not a live appointment calendar.

    Who am I speaking to in the chat?

    The assistant is clearly labelled as AI whenever it is connected. It can help you explore an email idea, but Sakina personally confirms all prices, timelines and deliverables. If chat is unavailable, you can reach her through LinkedIn.

    What happens to my messages and comments?

    Comments and display names are saved so other visitors can read them. You can delete your own comments from the same browser. Clearing browser data removes that access. The project planner stays in your browser until you choose to share your brief. When live AI chat is connected, messages are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to generate replies. Do not include passwords, customer lists or sensitive personal information.
